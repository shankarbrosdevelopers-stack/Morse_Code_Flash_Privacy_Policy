01

Introduction & Our Commitment

Welcome to Morse Code Flash, developed and published by ShankarBros. ("we", "us", or "our"). We believe that learning Morse code and using tactical flashlight communication should be private, safe, and transparent.

This Privacy Policy explains how Morse Code Flash operates, what permissions it requests, and why we do not collect, store, or sell any of your personal data.

Our Core Philosophy: Morse Code Flash does not require account creation, registration, email login, or phone numbers. All your practice sessions, learning progress, custom speeds, and streak statistics remain safely stored locally on your device.
02

Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

To provide Morse optical flashing, audio synthesis, and tactile feedback, the application requires specific Android permissions. Here is a clear breakdown of each permission:

Permission Hardware Purpose Privacy Guarantee
CAMERA / FLASHLIGHT Controls the device torch LED to emit Morse flashes and SOS optical signals. ZERO photos or videos recorded. The camera optical sensor is never opened or accessed. Only the flashlight torch hardware is toggled.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic vibrations when tapping the Morse telegraph key and receiving signals. Haptic motor strictly output-only. No sensor data captured.
INTERNET / NETWORK Allows Google AdMob to load ads and Google Play to verify optional In-App Billing upgrades. Core Morse tool, dictionary, and curriculum levels operate 100% offline without internet.
BILLING Enables purchasing optional Ad-Free Pro upgrades via Google Play Billing. Payment credentials are encrypted and processed exclusively by Google. ShankarBros. never sees or stores card details.
03

Third-Party Services & Libraries

The app integrates certified industry-standard third-party SDKs to support its functionality:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Free versions of the app display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads provided by Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may use pseudonymous device advertising identifiers (AD_ID) and diagnostic data to serve ads in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies. You can opt out of personalized ads via your Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Google Play In-App Billing (IAP): If you choose to unlock the Ad-Free Pro upgrade, the transaction is processed directly through Google Play Billing. We do not receive, store, or process any payment cards, billing addresses, or financial data.
04

Data Storage & Retention

All user game progress (curriculum level, mastered characters, streak count, audio frequency preferences, and theme selections) is stored locally on your device via Android's local preferences storage (Jetpack DataStore).

We do not maintain any private user databases or external servers. If you clear the app data or uninstall the application, all local statistics will be erased from your device.

05

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Morse Code Flash is an educational and utility app designed for all ages, including radio hobbyists, students, and emergency preparedness enthusiasts.

We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Our ad configurations adhere to Google Play Families Policy requirements.

06

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted to external servers, the risk of data compromise is strictly mitigated. We regularly review our security and keep third-party dependencies updated to the latest secure versions.
